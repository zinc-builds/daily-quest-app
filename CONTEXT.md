# Domain Glossary

## Daily Quest App

### Daily Goal
One of five active daily behaviors the user tracks. Has a name, description, category, target type, target value, XP reward, and active status.

### Daily Completion
A record that a daily goal was completed (or not) on a specific date. Stores completion state, value, note, and XP earned.

### Battlepass
A monthly reward track. Has a name, month, start/end dates, and total XP earned during the period.

### Reward Level
A tier in a battlepass with an XP threshold and a real-life reward. Can be unlocked and claimed independently.

### Perfect Day
A day on which all five active dailies were completed. Grants a bonus 100 XP.

### Monthly Review
A summary produced at the end of a battlepass period: XP earned/available, completion percentage, perfect days, best streak, per-daily completion rates, rewards unlocked/claimed.

### Target Type
How a daily is measured: `checkbox`, `count`, `minutes`, or `custom`.

### Reward Type
Category of real-life reward: `treat`, `purchase`, `experience`, `rest`, `social`, `custom`.

## UI Terms

### Marathon Aesthetic
The visual style of the app: black background, neon-lime accent, white text, sharp rectangular shapes, terminal-style brackets, monospace data labels.
